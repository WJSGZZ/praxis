Corrections retained in the process: the first baseline coefficients (surface 18, shell 5, body 12 W/(m² K)) and the 40.5°C upper limit were replaced after a literature-based derivation showed them at the low end of the range and the finer mesh rejected the limit; the baseline water amount rose from 10.48 L to 24.14 L as a result. A time-varying schedule found by constrained optimization was added because the constant-rate family left the gap to the energy bound open. An independent AI reviewer later found that the 41°C ceiling and the 1.5°C spread, applied to the inlet point-source cell, did not converge with the mesh and that conclusions built on them were mesh-specific; the limits were redefined outside a 0.15 m jet zone, every result was recomputed (that revision retained a 19.35 L schedule; a later audit rejected it on the finest grid), and the paper's wording on coefficients, tolerance and feasibility was corrected. This review is AI review, not review by a human. The correlations were checked against web summaries, not the textbook; the immersion study was read in full from the open-access author manuscript.

Structure and presentation of the later revision were informed by a brief reading of five MCM Outstanding papers on other problems (summary, contents, assumptions with reasons, roadmap, sensitivity, strengths and weaknesses); at that stage, no Outstanding paper or other solution to this problem had been read. During development the assistant did read COMAP's published judges' commentary on this problem once, then removed every change it had prompted (the citation, abstract, section and figure-caption edits); the demo is therefore not a fully blind case.
